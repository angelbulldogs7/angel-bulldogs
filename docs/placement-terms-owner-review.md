# Placement terms outline (owner review draft)

**Status:** Internal preparation checklist only. Not a public legal page. Not an executed contract. Transaction-specific amounts, dates, and party details must be completed and reviewed before any real deposit is requested.

## Confirmed public policy this draft must match

- Written reservation/sale terms are provided **before** payment.
- After a verbal agreement, a puppy may be **On hold** until a mutually agreed deposit deadline.
- When the deposit is received, status becomes **Reserved**.
- When purchase/placement is completed, status becomes **Sold**.
- If the deposit is not received by the agreed deadline, status returns to **Available** (no refund issue, because nothing was paid).
- Video calls and in-person appointments are offered **after** the deposit.
- The reservation deposit is returned if the buyer withdraws (including after a meeting) or if Angel Bulldogs cannot complete the placement.
- Returning a reservation deposit does **not** create an automatic refund policy for a completed purchase.
- No additional genetic/health guarantee is approved for website marketing.
- Rehoming help or possible return is considered case by case, with no unconditional take-back or purchase-price refund promise.

## Fields to complete per transaction

| Item | Draft treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Parties and puppy | Private seller/buyer details and the chosen puppy name/identifier. Human names and home address are not website content. |
| Price and reservation deposit | Blank fields for amounts and payment method, discussed privately. Do not invent numbers. |
| Temporary hold | Mutually agreed deposit due date/time and how payment acknowledgment is confirmed. |
| Status language | On hold before payment → Reserved after receipt → Sold after completion. |
| Refund | Return of reservation deposit for buyer withdrawal or seller inability to complete placement; transaction logistics agreed before payment. |
| Meetings | Video/in-person appointments follow the deposit and are scheduled directly. |
| Final arrangements | Agreed balance, payment timing, local pickup / airport meeting / owner delivery, and any actual costs. |
| Records and registration | Handover records and KSS / optional AKC application distinction matching the website. |
| Additional guarantee | Leave blank / state none advertised; do not invent warranty, replacement, reimbursement cap, or statutory-rights waiver. |
| Support and rehoming | Personal ongoing support; rehoming assistance or possible return considered case by case. |
| Review and acceptance | Mark the completed document as reviewed for the actual transaction and applicable requirements before use. |

## Short reservation summary template (not a substitute agreement)

> We have agreed on a temporary hold for [puppy] until [agreed deposit deadline]. Before payment, we will provide the written terms and confirm the purchase price, deposit, and arrangements with you. Once the deposit is received, [puppy] will be reserved. Video calls and in-person appointments are arranged after the deposit. We return the reservation deposit if you decide not to proceed or if we cannot complete the placement.

## Owner action before a real deposit

1. Complete the blanks for that buyer and puppy.
2. Review the document for the applicable transaction requirements.
3. Provide the written terms before accepting payment.
4. Update the website status manually (Available → On hold → Reserved → Sold) when each stage is real.
