# Puppy follow-up screening guide

Owner-facing conversation checklist for Angel Bulldogs. Use this **after** a short public puppy inquiry and the first personal reply about availability, pricing, and fit interest.

This is not a public form, applicant portal, or automated approval system. An unlisted URL alone is not private. Keep placement decisions with the owner.

## Before detailed screening

Discuss these items in the first personal reply when possible:

1. Current availability for the puppy or interest choice they selected
2. Confirmed puppy price
3. Applicable delivery costs, if delivery is being considered

Do not ask for a budget field on the public inquiry. Do not invent financing, discounts, deposit terms, or guarantees.

## Conversation checklist

### 1. Housing and permission

- Residence type (house, apartment, townhome, other)
- Own, rent, or another arrangement
- If renting or otherwise restricted: are pets permitted? Yes / No / Need to confirm
- Clear any “not applicable” answer when renting; only ask permission when it is relevant

### 2. Household

- Number of adults
- Children and broad age ranges (no children’s names)
- Whether the household is aligned about bringing home a puppy

### 3. Other pets

- Any current pets? Yes / No
- If yes: species, approximate ages, and compatibility notes with a French Bulldog

### 4. Daily care

- Primary caregiver
- Typical daily routine
- Typical time the puppy may be alone, using clear options such as:
  - Rarely — someone is usually home
  - Usually under 4 hours
  - About 4–6 hours
  - About 6–8 hours
  - More than 8 hours on some days
  - Varies week to week
- What arrangements exist for midday breaks, workdays, travel, or unexpected absences
- These answers support a conversation; they are not automatic rejection rules and are not recommendations for leaving a puppy unattended

### 5. Experience and support

- Previous French Bulldog or dog experience
- What guidance would help them prepare
- First-time owners remain eligible for a personal discussion

### 6. Long-term care

Discuss rather than quiz:

- Companionship expectations
- Training approach
- Temperature management for French Bulldogs
- Veterinary planning
- Readiness for ongoing expenses

### 7. Matching and logistics

- If no specific puppy was chosen: sex or coat preferences, if any
- If a specific puppy was chosen: do not require contradictory sex/coat answers; clarify only if preferences conflict with that puppy
- Timing to welcome a puppy
- Chicago pickup vs nationwide delivery
- Delivery details only when delivery is relevant (ZIP and other logistics can be collected here)

### 8. Breeding-rights follow-up (only if requested)

- Experience or program context
- Reminder that breeding rights are case by case and not automatically included
- Follow the owner’s existing approval process; do not open a public breeder questionnaire on the first inquiry

## After screening

- A successful inquiry is not an approved application or reservation
- The owner makes the placement decision
- Reservation and paperwork follow the existing verified private process

## Measurement notes (no analytics vendor installed)

If analytics is added later, track only aggregate events such as:

- Puppy inquiry page views / starts
- Confirmed successful inquiry submissions

Do **not** send names, emails, phone numbers, location text, or message content to analytics.

Until then, compare completed inquiries with how many become serious placement conversations.
